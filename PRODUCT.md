# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the single owner and operator of the deployed service. They manage their own Telegram sessions, choose a source and destination, and monitor one forwarding job at a time.

## Product Purpose

Provide a private browser-based control panel for signing in to Telegram and forwarding newly received media from a restricted source channel to a destination the operator controls. Success means the operator can deploy the service on their own server, complete the workflow without desktop access, and understand whether forwarding is healthy.

## Positioning

A focused, self-hosted Telegram forwarding console that keeps setup, control, and operational status in one private web interface.

## Brand Personality

Soft, calm, and precise. The interface takes its pastel pink, lavender, and aqua atmosphere from Hyacine in Honkai: Star Rail while retaining the clarity and restraint expected from an operational tool.

## Anti-references

Avoid game-like or gacha-style chrome, excessive effects, visual clutter, and low-contrast sweetness that makes operational status difficult to read.

## Design Principles

- Keep the active forwarding state and primary next action obvious.
- Make sensitive account actions deliberate and clearly explained.
- Use the pastel theme for atmosphere, never at the expense of contrast or status clarity.
- Prefer familiar web controls and short workflows over decorative interaction.
- Show useful empty, loading, success, and error states in context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, complete keyboard and visible-focus support, status communication that does not rely on color alone, touch-friendly controls, and reduced-motion support.
